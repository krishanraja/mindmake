## The one sentence (approved by Krish, 18 November 2026)

About him (Boardy, hosts, intros):

> **Most leaders now ask one AI chat about everything and get roughly the answer everyone else gets; Krish spends 30 days building them an AI Brain that lays out each big decision, shows its sources and carries their standards into every tool they use.**

By him (LinkedIn, his own voice): "Most leaders now ask one AI chat about everything and get roughly the answer everyone else gets. I spend 30 days building them an AI Brain that lays out each big decision, shows its sources and carries their standards into every tool they use."

By the company (mindmake.co, "we"): "Most leaders now ask one AI chat about everything and get roughly the answer everyone else gets. We spend 30 days building you an AI Brain that lays out each big decision, shows its sources and carries your standards into every tool you use."

Why it holds: it answers "I already use Claude for that" with what a single chat cannot do, and it frames the gap as a common habit rather than a personal failing. "Roughly the answer everyone else gets" is Krish's own diagnosis, recorded in ctrl-mindmake `docs/current/sales-pack/positioning.md` as a view with no number; never put a figure on it.

## The diagnosis, in Krish's words (18 November 2026)

"Most leaders delegate the AI plan to the non visionary person like revops or IT, and make unrealistic demands of it. Most cant keep up, feel behind, and feel like they will go out date. Lots of leaders are waiting for the AI to get better and take work off their plate and bring them a list of things to do, which will put them in the camp of people that become redundant as their intellectual IP gets drained by AI over time and they do not learn to use the machine for their own benefit. I work with leaders to solve that."

And on the urgency (18 November 2026): it must "drive urgency for the human who is just asking one claude session everything, not really realizing if their ai tool just guessed back at them, and their complex decisions are not visualized, classified, and presented to them in a way they can amplfy their critical thinking, validate their ideas and choices and propogate their vision, standards, taste and judgement (their AI Brain) through their work."

Public form: urgency and ambition, never fear and never a put-down. The diagnosis goes in the problem section of a page and the second line of an intro, framed as something a leader can avoid.

## Who it is for

- **Leaders who are** curious, determined to evolve, imaginative, with strong standards and a clear north star (Krish, 18 Nov 2026).
- **Businesses:** fast-growing companies building the next era of creative and media technology (Krish, 18 Nov 2026), within the canon's horizon of PE or VC backed media, adtech and data businesses (`../00_NORTH_STAR.md`). Recording the newer words is decision D9.
- **Not a fit:** someone who cannot make the decision, or who wants AI training, a certificate or a list of tasks to automate (`../00_NORTH_STAR.md`).
