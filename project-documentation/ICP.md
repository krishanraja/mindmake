# Ideal Customer Profile

**Last Updated:** 2026-03-03

---

## Primary ICPs

### ICP 1: The Builder
**Who:** Leaders who want to build alongside AI — prototype, ship, create leverage.

**Titles:** CEO, CPO, CTO-adjacent leaders, Founder, VP of Product
**Characteristics:** 10+ years leadership, comfortable getting hands-on, wants to ship fast
**Nervous Decisions:**
- "What should I build vs buy?"
- "Which tools do I actually commit to?"
- "How do I multiply my strongest edge with AI?"
- "How do I prototype without a full technical team?"

**Sprint Fit:**
- 4-Week Sprint: Single build-vs-buy decision
- 90-Day Sprint: Multiple systems deployed + roadmap

**What They Become:** A leader who builds alongside AI. Working systems, not slide decks. They ship.

---

### ICP 2: The Orchestrator
**Who:** Leaders who want to set standards and make clean decisions without getting buried in technical details.

**Titles:** CEO, COO, CFO, GM, CCO, CMO
**Characteristics:** 10+ years leadership, P&L accountability, delegates execution but owns outcomes
**Nervous Decisions:**
- "Which vendors do we commit to?"
- "How do I evaluate AI vendor promises vs reality?"
- "What's my AI boundary for this year?"
- "My board wants an AI strategy. I don't know where to start."
- "Everyone on my team is using different tools. It's chaos."

**Sprint Fit:**
- 4-Week Sprint: Single vendor or governance decision
- 90-Day Sprint: Full AI governance framework + team alignment

**What They Become:** A leader who sets standards and makes clean decisions. Board-level confidence. No more theatre.

### Common Traits (Both ICPs)

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Company Size** | Series B to late-stage, $10M-$500M revenue, 50-500 employees |
| **Budget Authority** | $500K+ budget authority |

| **Motivation** | Done being sold AI. Ready to use it. |

## Anti-ICPs

- Technical teams (engineers, data scientists) — they don't need decisions, they need code
- Early-stage founders with no revenue — they need to ship product, not AI strategy
- Individual contributors — Mindmaker is for decision-makers
- Leaders who want training/certification — we don't teach, we help you decide
- Leaders looking for someone to do it for them — we help you decide, not do it for you

---

## Qualification Signals

### Strong Fit (Start 4-Week Sprint)
- Has one specific AI anxiety they can name
- Board or peers are asking about AI
- Has been pitched by multiple vendors
- Wants to make a decision, not learn about AI
- Says "I should understand this but I don't"

### Strongest Fit (Start 90-Day Sprint)
- Has 2-3 competing AI decisions
- Needs to align a team on AI direction
- Wants deployed systems, not just a plan
- Has budget and authority to act on decisions
- Board presentation coming up

### Not a Fit
- "Just exploring AI" with no urgency
- Technical team wanting implementation help
- Looking for a tool recommendation (we do trade-off analysis, not recommendations)
- Wants ongoing retainer (we do sprints, then you're independent)
