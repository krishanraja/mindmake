# Mindmaker Homepage Motion Storyboard

Status: proposed for homepage mock V3

## Motion rule

Movement must show that evidence is arriving, a choice is being made, or ownership is passing to the visitor. Decorative motion is secondary and must never change the meaning of the page.

The page keeps normal browser scrolling. There is no scroll lock, forced timeline or full-screen chapter system.

## Signature build 1: The working evidence wall

Previous production behaviour:

- Particle field moves continuously.
- The hero statement changes every five seconds.
- Motion creates energy but also changes the proposition.

V2 behaviour:

- Photographs and CTRL footage are already assembled.
- One image slowly changes crop.

V3 sequence:

1. The wordmark and main sentence appear immediately. No loading delay.
2. The round-table photograph settles into place from the right.
3. The stage photograph is placed over it like a physical print.
4. A real CTRL crop rises from the lower edge and begins moving.
5. A short paper note lands last: “The tools and memory stay with your team.”
6. Hovering or tapping CTRL expands it into a focused drawer with one clear explanation.

Meaning:

- Real work, product evidence and the handback promise form one picture.

Success check:

- The visitor can still read the full proposition before the build finishes.
- The sequence completes in under 1.8 seconds.
- Reduced motion shows the final composition immediately.

## Signature build 2: Decision Underneath

Previous production behaviour:

- The Diagnosis Room enriches a company, supports voice and creates a structured brief.
- A second nervous-decision input duplicates the entry.

V2 behaviour:

- Three fixed sample pressures appear after a timer.
- The selected pressure swaps to a fixed result.

V3 sequence:

1. A company domain is entered or inferred from an email domain.
2. The company mark and Mindmaker mark resolve together.
3. Small source marks arrive beside a plain business description.
4. Two or three pressure paths draw from specific facts.
5. The visitor taps the closest pressure.
6. Unselected paths fold away.
7. The selected path widens and reorganises into:
   - the decision underneath;
   - why it may matter now;
   - the likely cause;
   - one useful 21-day test;
   - the most important fact Mindmaker cannot know outside.
8. Email and booking actions appear only after the result is readable.

Meaning:

- The visitor sees the difference between a sourced inference and a chat response.

Success check:

- Every displayed fact has a source mark.
- The visitor gives no more than two inputs before value.
- A mobile visitor completes the path one-handed.
- Selection receives one restrained haptic response.
- Reduced motion uses direct state replacement and keeps the evidence hierarchy.

## Signature build 3: The business brain

Previous production behaviour:

- Two Doors, Operator's Edge and Operator's Brief separately show CTRL, agents, memory and live data.
- Flip and card interactions create discovery.

V2 behaviour:

- Four tabs replace text and show two static CTRL images.

V3 sequence:

1. Four plain business areas sit around an empty shared centre:
   - product;
   - price;
   - place in the market;
   - team.
2. Choosing an area brings in one real decision, one CTRL crop and one piece of useful outside data.
3. The evidence connects to a shared memory spine.
4. Moving to another area keeps the spine and replaces only the decision-specific parts.
5. A “what stays with you” control reveals the working tools, decision record and next test.

Meaning:

- CTRL is not a separate assistant. It is the structure that helps the work last.

Success check:

- Each area explains a business result before a technical feature.
- The visitor can inspect any area with keyboard, tap or pointer.
- No decorative network graph appears without labelled meaning.

## Supporting delight

### Pressure cards

Retain the production tap-to-flip discovery. The front names the pressure. The reverse names the decision Mindmaker would test. Remove repeated sales buttons.

### Proof marks

Attendance logos remain still by default. On first view, a single rule draws beneath the qualifier and the three media marks resolve from outline to full monochrome. They never look like clients.

### Case files

The result grid is always scannable. Opening a story expands it in place into problem, decision, work and outcome. The case number moves to the margin and the result is stamped once. No carousel.

### Sprint handback

As the three weeks enter view, the decision, test and working system are placed on one shared line. At the end, that line moves from the Mindmaker side to the client side. This is a visual handback, not a generic timeline fill.

### Krish evidence drawer

One photograph or CTRL crop may open into a short video, audio clip or annotated artefact. It stays optional and closes with Escape.

### Owl

The owl appears at one meaningful transition and at the final action. It does not float continuously around the page.

## Motion tokens

- Immediate response: 120 to 180 milliseconds.
- Small placement: 220 to 360 milliseconds.
- Section build: 600 to 900 milliseconds.
- Hero sequence: no more than 1.8 seconds in total.
- Result reorganisation: no more than 1.1 seconds after selection.
- Use transform, opacity and clip paths. Avoid layout-thrashing width and height animation where possible.
- Use one easing family with a firm start and soft landing.
- Sound is off by default and never carries unique meaning.

## Regression checks

V3 fails if:

- Any section waits for motion before its main text can be read.
- Motion repeats every time the visitor scrolls past.
- The company read resembles fake typing.
- The result resembles a dashboard, score gauge or chatbot.
- Mobile motion pushes the selected control away from the visitor's thumb.
- Reduced motion removes source, state or ownership information.
- A build adds delight without adding meaning.
