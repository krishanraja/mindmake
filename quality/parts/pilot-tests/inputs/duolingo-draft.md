**Company:** Duolingo, Inc. | **Writer:** Chief Financial Officer | **Use case:** Results commentary

We reported third quarter results on 3 November, and I want to take the board through the main points. Daily active users reached 64.9 million, up 29% year on year, which accelerates from 23% growth in the second quarter. Paid subscribers rose 14% to 13.1 million. Total bookings grew 12% to $316.1 million, and revenue grew 14% to $309.0 million. Net income was $27.2 million, well below the $292.2 million of a year ago, which included a $222.7 million tax benefit. Adjusted EBITDA was $78.7 million, a 25.5% margin. We ended the quarter with $1.6 billion in cash and short-term investments. Our internal cohort data show that learners who use Video Call in their first 30 days renew at a rate six points higher than those who do not. On the outlook, we now expect full-year Adjusted EBITDA of $323 million, a 26.5% margin, which is $3 million above our previous guidance. We also expect to finish the year at the high end of our bookings and revenue growth ranges. Our priorities remain teaching better and growing users, and we are leaning into monetisation gradually. I am happy to take questions before the next board meeting.
