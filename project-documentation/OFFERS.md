## 1. The AI Decision Cohort

### Position

The quarterly decision room for senior leaders wrestling with AI decisions. Not a course. Not 1:1 advisory. A peer cohort with real structure, a real finish line, and a board-ready output.

### For

Senior operators (CEO, COO, CFO, CPO, CCO, CMO, GM, VP-level) with one nervous AI decision they've been avoiding and the budget authority to act once resolved.

### Format

- 10–15 seats per cohort, 4 cohorts per year
- ~5 weeks elapsed: 3 weeks async (readings, short prompts, peer debate threads) + 3 live sessions (90 minutes each)
- Live sessions are intimate, Chatham House rules, facilitated by Krish
- Each member picks one nervous decision at the start and must produce a board-ready position by the end

### Arc

| Phase | Theme | What happens |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **Week 0** | Onboarding | Name the decision. Submit a one-paragraph brief. Get matched with a sparring partner. |
| **Week 1 — async** | Mind Set (Clarity) | Cut noise. Identify what the decision is actually about, not what the vendor deck says it's about. |
| **Week 1 — live 1** | Decision framing | Each member frames their decision to the room. Peer pushback sharpens the question. |
| **Week 2 — async** | Mind Map (Leverage) | Map options. Trade-off analysis. Rule out what doesn't matter. |
| **Week 2 — live 2** | Options and constraints | Work through the hardest trade-off live. Krish facilitates; peers press-test. |
| **Week 3 — async** | Mind Make (Direction) | Draft the board-ready position. Decision memo format, one page. |
| **Week 3 — live 3** | Defence and commitment | Each member defends their position to the cohort. Commit. Ship. |

### What you walk out with

- A board-ready position on your nervous decision (1-page decision memo)
- Trade-off analysis with options named and reasons for rejection documented
- A peer network of 10–15 operators at comparable seniority
- A framework for the next nervous decision (Mind Set → Mind Map → Mind Make)

### Example nervous decisions (cohort)

- "Do we build or buy our AI stack for [specific workflow]?"
- "Which AI vendor do we commit to by end of Q and what's the exit plan?"
- "What AI governance framework do we ship next quarter without slowing the team?"
- "How do I articulate our AI strategy to the board in plain language?"
- "What do we replace vs what do we empower in our org design?"

### What it is not

- Not a course. No certification. No lecture videos.
- Not 1:1 advisory. Krish is in the room but the cohort is the product.
- Not a vendor showcase. Zero vendor pitch.

## 2. The Signal Session

### Position

The enterprise entry point. One day, one room, one commercial thesis for a company that has built AI capability and can't translate it into revenue predictably.

### For

Founders, CEOs, CCOs, CROs, CPOs at companies that have shipped AI product or AI-enabled capability in the last 12 months and are seeing erratic pricing, deal size, or GTM traction.

### Format

- One intensive day (approximately 6 hours in-session), on-site or fully remote
- 2 weeks pre-work: questionnaire, document review, commercial data request
- Written thesis delivered within 5 business days after the session
- Krish delivers personally, no associates

### What the day covers

1. **Commercial diagnosis.** Pricing, positioning, packaging, ICP, sales motion — where is the friction?
2. **AI-specific commercial translation.** What does the AI capability enable that the existing GTM isn't built for?
3. **Prioritisation.** Of everything broken, what matters in the next 90 days?
4. **Decision gate.** Does the Revenue Architecture engagement make sense? If not, we say so and you leave with the thesis.

### What you walk out with

- A prioritized commercial thesis (written, ~5–10 pages) delivered within 5 business days
- A named list of the 3 commercial problems to solve in the next 90 days
- A clear read on whether the Revenue Architecture is warranted, with scope estimate if yes
- The written thesis is yours to keep whether or not you proceed

### What it is not

- Not a sales pitch for the Revenue Architecture. If the answer is "you don't need it," that's the answer.
- Not implementation or deployment. No production IT.
- Not a multi-session engagement. One day, one thesis, done.

## 3. The Revenue Architecture

### Position

The flagship. A commercial rebuild for a company whose AI capability deserves better positioning, pricing, packaging, and GTM than it currently has. Informed by an operator running a real agentic organisation, not a theorist.

### For

Enterprise buyers (typically $10M–$1B+ revenue) who have:
- Real AI capability in market or launching imminently
- Budget authority for $60–100k (50/50 payment)
- Leadership willing to act on the output in the following 90 days
- An 8–12 week window for the engagement

### Format

- 8–12 weeks elapsed (scope-dependent)
- Kickoff workshop on-site; remaining cadence weekly working sessions plus async deliverables
- Krish-led; no associate model
- Fixed scope defined at Signal Session or kickoff — no scope creep

### What gets rebuilt

| Area | Deliverable |
|------|-------------|
| **Positioning** | New positioning statement, category framing, differentiation narrative |
| **Pricing** | Pricing architecture with tiers, value metric, packaging, discount policy |
| **Packaging** | SKU / offer structure aligned to buyer journey |
| **GTM motion** | Sales motion map, qualifying framework, sales enablement artefacts |
| **ICP refresh** | Updated ICP definition with qualification signals |
| **Commercial roadmap** | 12-month plan with quarterly gates, owners, and success metrics |
| **Board narrative** | The "AI → revenue" story leadership can defend internally and externally |

### What you walk out with

- Positioning, pricing, and packaging documents — board-ready
- GTM motion map and sales enablement artefacts
- 12-month commercial roadmap with gates, owners, and metrics
- Krish is in the room with the commercial team for the entire engagement

### What it is not

- Not implementation. No building products. No running campaigns.
- Not ongoing. Finish line is hard. Post-engagement support only via separate inquiry.
- Not a fractional CAIO / CRO arrangement. Operator guidance during the engagement only.
- Not production IT. Explicitly out of scope.
