# Mindmake messaging and FAQ

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

## One line

Mindmake helps leaders put their best judgement to work with AI.

## Short explanation

Krish brings more than 17 years across data, technology and product strategy, plus two years building deeply with AI. He helps a leader keep what they know close, bring the right facts and past choices into view, and turn a hard choice into a working tool their team can use.

There are two public ways to begin: `Build Your AI Brain` and `Build Your AI GTM`. Either may lead to one privately priced 30-day proof.

## Main action

`Start here`

The visitor gives a company website. Mindmake gives a useful read, tailored pressure choices and a recommendation before asking for contact details. After one easy question, the visitor may request the full private brief by email and receives the branded proposal once a six-digit code confirms the address. There is no public diary or direct Calendly path.

## Common questions

### Do I need a finished brief?

No. An unclear problem is a valid place to start. The first job is to find a useful place to begin.

### Is this AI training?

No. The work uses AI, but the outcome is a more capable leader or a better business result.

### Is this an automation service?

No. The work does not start with a list of tasks to automate. It starts with the expertise, judgement and taste in the business and the commercial result that needs to change.

### What do we keep?

The working first version, the judgement and evidence behind it, and a private CTRL-backed system that keeps the work useful.

### How much does it cost?

The scope and fee are agreed privately after the brief. The website does not publish a price.

### Why Krish?

He combines long commercial experience with real AI systems he has built and run. He does not hand the work to a junior team. The goal is to make the client more capable, not more dependent.

### Will the site add me to the publication?

No. Publication interest is a separate, unticked choice. It records interest only and does not subscribe or import anyone.

### Is the private brief email live?

Yes. Gate E closed on 27 August 2026. A verified work email receives the branded proposal with the brief attached, and every failure path keeps the complete local download. No sales emails follow automatically.

## Words to avoid

Do not use AI literacy, AI fluency, prompt engineering, leveraging AI, AI-powered, future of work, chief of staff, productivity or assistant as the public frame.

Use British English, common words and short sentences. Do not use em dashes. Keep approved quotes verbatim.
