# Mindmake buyer

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

The four buyer groups are:

1. Founder.
2. CEO.
3. CRO, the leader responsible for revenue.
4. Strategy leader.

The person must own, or be able to move, the decision and the business result behind it.

## Best-fit moment

- Faster startups may be taking the market.
- Growth is possible, but the product, price, positioning or people is holding it back.
- AI has changed an important product, sales or company decision.
- The leader knows something is wrong but cannot yet name the real problem.

The first sales wedge is media, data, creative, publishing and content businesses because Krish has strong proof and relationships there. This is a sales focus, not the public brand position. Coaching and consulting businesses can also be a strong fit.

## Poor fit

- The person cannot move the decision.
- The request is only for training, certification or a list of task automations.
- The work needs production information technology delivery rather than a commercial decision.
- The buyer wants an open-ended retainer or fractional job.
- There is no decision, business result or person who owns it.
