# Mindmake canon

Last updated: 26 August 2026.

This is the current internal source of truth for the Mindmake business and public website. It supersedes older offer, positioning, CTA and naming guidance wherever they conflict. `DECISIONS_LOG.md` keeps the history. `REBUILD_STATE.md` keeps the current delivery state.

## The business

Mindmake is a principal-led AI and commercial strategy practice run by Krish Raja.

It helps a leader use AI to extend their judgment, taste and expertise, then turn that stronger capability into a better business result. The work may improve the leader, the product, the price, the message, the route to market or the company structure that supports them.

Mindmake is not an automation consultancy. It does not start with a list of repetitive tasks. It starts with the value of a leader's time, the decisions only they can make and the capability the business should retain after the work.

## The simple public model

The site uses two doors because they meet non-technical buyers in language they already understand:

1. **Build Your AI Brain.** Make your judgement, standards, memory and trusted context easier to use. Fill a real gap without giving away the human spark that put you in the seat.
2. **Build Your AI GTM.** Rework product, price, position, sales and team choices as AI changes what customers can do and what they will pay for.

These are not two unrelated products. Either may be the hook, and either may lead to the other when the work calls for it.

## The value ladder

AI can create three levels of value:

1. **Help me do the task.** Faster drafting, admin or analysis.
2. **Bring me what matters.** The right information arrives before the leader has to search for it.
3. **Extend what I can do.** The leader gains a working memory, a new way to see and act, or a system that keeps improving their decisions.

Mindmake is built for level three. Levels one and two may support the work, but they are not the premium promise.

Time saved is the setup. The larger question is whose time comes back, what they put it into and whether the new capability keeps compounding.

## The working-understanding distinction

Consultants, AI models, single-job tools and all-in-one systems can all do useful work. Mindmake does not need to dismiss them or pretend they have no value.

The risk is that the work moves while the leader's own understanding stays still. When all the learning lives with other people and tools, the leader becomes less able to test the work, ask the next question and make the call.

The simple public hinge is: **You can hand over the work. Not the understanding.**

Mindmake is not a fifth tool in that list. It works with the leader to build enough understanding for them to direct the help, keep their judgement current and decide what matters. The private long-term risk is capability loss. Public copy should express agency, confidence and the value of staying able to lead, not threaten the reader with becoming obsolete.

## Best-fit buyers

The strongest buyer is a founder, principal, portfolio owner, investor or senior commercial leader who can move a decision and has enough influence for the result to matter.

Strong situations include:

- a healthy or fast-growing AI company whose product, price, position or company design needs to keep up with its market;
- an established internet, software, media, data, content, coaching or advisory business whose old commercial rules have stopped working;
- a well-connected principal whose scarce time is spread across several ventures, relationships or decisions;
- a leader who wants an AI chief of staff or durable AI brain that makes their own judgment easier to use;
- a portfolio owner who wants more high-value time for companies, relationships and decisions, not simply a smaller inbox.

Do not write only for businesses in decline. Ambition, speed and the chance to build a stronger company are as important as competitive pressure.

## The paid starting point

Work starts with a paid 30-day proof. There is no public monetary price.

The proof chooses one important decision or capability, builds a working first version, uses it on real work and leaves behind something the client can keep using. It is not a month of discovery followed by a report.

The first month must be:

- tightly bounded;
- useful on its own;
- built from repeatable research, capture and synthesis;
- honest about what still needs human judgment;
- strong enough for the client to decide whether a longer relationship is worth it.

The proof counts as working when the client says it is useful in practice and is willing to stand behind that view. A strong testimonial or case study is the clearest supporting evidence, subject to consent.

## Continuation

The best work may continue for three months or longer. The continuation earns its place by finding and building the next useful opportunity, not by making the client dependent on Krish.

The standalone first month costs more than one third of a three-month relationship. If the client continues, the proof is treated as month one of the longer relationship and the commercial terms recognise the lower multi-month unit rate.

Terms may flex for a rare opportunity that brings meaningful equity, access, relationships, content, case-study rights or another compounding asset. Any such trade is explicit. Exposure on its own is not payment.

## Capacity and economics

- No more than two new 30-day proofs run at once.
- Each proof uses no more than 24 to 30 hours of Krish's time.
- A third paying client must already be in a lighter continuation phase.
- The private target is work worth well above $300 per hour once delivery and compounding value are considered.
- Broad unmanaged implementation, daily embedded availability and work that cannot fit the envelope are custom, partner-supported or declined.

No public price, discount, availability claim or start date may be inferred from these internal rules.

## The compounding rule

Hands-on building is good when it also strengthens an asset that compounds for Mindmake. The preferred assets are:

- reusable methods and delivery systems;
- anonymised benchmarks, trends, shifts and comparisons between moments in time;
- relationships and access;
- content and proof;
- owned intelligence that makes the next engagement sharper.

Commodity agent libraries are not the strategy. The stronger long-term asset is an anonymised record of how AI use, value and monetisation change over time.

Client files, private data and client-specific outputs remain protected. Reuse applies to methods and properly anonymised patterns, not confidential material.

## Proof and product

CTRL is Mindmake's product and evidence layer, hosted at `ctrl.mindmake.co`. It can show that the work keeps memory, sources, checks and the human call visible. It is not a third public offer.

The personal contact system is a strong example of level-three value. It brings together relationships from separate sources so a leader can find the right person by asking naturally. Public proof must not reveal private contacts, raw records or unverified counts. Vendor names and totals may appear only after a current evidence check and explicit approval.

Product footage stays clean. Do not draw over changing interfaces. Abstract working marks may explain a decision or result beside the footage.

## Public conversion path

There is no public diary link and no `Book a fit call` CTA.

The main public action is `Start here`.

The intended live path is:

1. The visitor gives a company website.
2. Mindmake shows what it can establish and suggests a few likely problems.
3. The visitor makes one easy choice.
4. Mindmake shows a useful preview, including what AI can carry, what should remain human and what a 30-day proof could test.
5. The visitor gives their email to keep the full private brief.
6. The visitor receives the designed brief. Krish receives the same inputs plus the richer company and fit context.
7. Krish decides whether to reply and, if useful, invites the person to a private conversation.

The safe default is deliberately narrower than the intended live path. It stops after the useful preview and lets the visitor download the full brief without giving an email. The version-two private hand-off now exists in source behind `VITE_MINDMAKE_BRIEF_HANDOFF_ENABLED=true`, but it is not deployed or preview-verified. The browser sends only the email, company domain and allowlisted choice IDs. The server owns the company research, recommendation, visitor brief, Krish's fit summary, verification code and delivery attempts. The old contact endpoint is never a fallback.

The flag stays off until the preview database migration, Edge Function, visitor email, operator email, origin rules, secret configuration, privacy wording and retention process have passed the complete release contract. Source code and passing local tests are not evidence that a private brief was delivered.

The preferred second question is: **If you got more of your best time back, where would you put it?**

Suggested answers:

- Grow this business.
- Help more companies.
- Build my AI skill.
- Make room for important decisions.

The newsletter choice is separate, optional and unticked. There is no automated personalised watch or recurring nurture sequence in the current build.

## Mobile interaction doctrine

Mobile is a recomposed experience, not a smaller desktop page.

- The wordmark, opening act and later sections share one content edge. Phone layouts must not invent a new left alignment from section to section.
- Fit-screen scenes use the visible canvas below the fixed header. Safe areas, the mobile browser bar and the on-screen keyboard are part of that canvas.
- At 900 pixels wide or below, the measured consent notice becomes top chrome. Its exact rendered height is reserved for the header, open menu, route opening canvas and frozen gateway. Accepting the notice removes that reserve and restores the full canvas.
- Every control has a touch target of at least 44 by 44 pixels. A narrow icon may sit inside that target, but the tappable area may not shrink to the artwork.
- The mobile menu fills the remaining viewport below the header. Opening it locks page scroll. Escape closes it, and focus returns to the menu control.
- Short landscape layouts become a purposeful two-pane composition or a horizontal snap rail. They do not squash a portrait design until it technically fits.
- A horizontal rail reveals part of the next card, supports touch swiping and keeps its controls in a stable place. The counter must show the last card explicitly when the rail reaches its physical end.
- Scroll-linked motion moves back when the visitor scrolls back. Reduced-motion mode keeps the same meaning without relying on animation.
- Headings and body copy must not form aggressive narrow stacks, leave accidental orphan words, truncate or disappear behind a clipping container.
- Video is user-controlled and uses `preload="none"` unless a separately tested experience requires otherwise. A page must remain understandable before any video plays.
- The starting brief responds to `visualViewport` changes, the on-screen keyboard and safe-area insets. Back, close, browser history and Escape must all behave predictably without retaining private input from a closed journey.
- Software checks do not replace physical hardware checks. iOS Safari and Android Chrome remain release gates.
