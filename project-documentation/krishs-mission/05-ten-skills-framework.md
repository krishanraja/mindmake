# Proposal: the ten skills of the post-AI leader

Status: Proposed, awaiting Krish (decision D4). Source: "Human Skills for Employability, 2026-2028", a research synthesis Krish shared on 17 November 2026 (kept privately; not committed here). Its evidence register is listed in `06-evidence.md`.

Krish, 18 November 2026: "the skills MD file I gave you proves that this mission and product increases at least 7 of the 10 skills everyone agrees will be the future of a post AI human leader. Thats the real clutch surely".

## The honest count: seven of ten

| Skill | What builds it | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| S01 Judgement and critical thinking | Thinking a call through: your own rules asked back, every fact sourced | Yes |
| S02 Practical AI fluency | Directing AI from your own Brain, inside your own tools | Yes |
| S05 Learning agility and adaptability | "Getting better at" in the Brain; Today asks "Did this change what you will do?" | Yes |
| S06 Problem framing | A hard call kept as one choice with several parts | Yes |
| S07 Data literacy | Where each fact came from; "Check a number before I rely on it" | Yes |
| S08 Workflow design and automation | The 30 days ends with a working system the leader runs | Yes |
| S10 Initiative and accountable execution | One decision, a first version, used on real work; "You decide" | Yes |
| S03 Deep competence in a field | Makes the leader's standards explicit; does not teach the field | Partly |
| S04 Communication and influence | Writes in their own voice; that is not building their skill | Partly |
| S09 Collaboration and conflict resolution | One person, one Brain | No |

## Two cautions before it goes public

1. **Nobody "agrees" on the ten.** The synthesis says its order is "an editorial judgement... not a ranking measured by a single study". Quote its sources (`06-evidence.md`), never a consensus or the synthesis by title. Never say "research ranks judgement first".
2. **It argues against Mindmake in one place.** Its row for managers and senior leaders prioritises judgement, communication, collaboration and accountable delivery. Communication and collaboration are the two a Brain builds least. Own it: a Brain builds the thinking skills; the people skills stay the leader's.

## How to use it

- **Published as Mindmake's own framework:** "The ten skills of the post-AI leader: our read of the employer research", with every source and its limit. Once it is Mindmake's published framework, "a Brain builds seven of the ten" is an honest, ownable claim.
- **For partners** (coaches, learning and development leads, business schools, CEO peer groups): their currency is skills. This is the lead for the partner lane (`08-partners-speaking-boardy.md`).
- **On stage:** "The ten skills of the post-AI leader, and the seven your AI can build with you."
- **Beside "Who decided?":** the audit already uses the synthesis's four evidence levels (aware, supported, independent, adapting), never a score. The levels are its editorial tool, never called validated.
- **In the pitch:** the named skills go in the second line (`02-positioning-and-pitch.md`), not the count.
