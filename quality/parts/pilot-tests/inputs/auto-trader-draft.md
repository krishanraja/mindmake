# TEST DRAFT, written by Mindmake from public sources, not by the company

**Company:** Auto Trader Group plc | **Writer:** Chief Financial Officer | **Use case:** Monthly board pack

This pack covers the half year results we published on 5 November, together with the main operating indicators. Group revenue declined 1% to £313.4m, while revenue excluding vehicle and accessory sales rose marginally to £301.8m. Group operating profit fell 1% to £198.5m, and the margin held at 63%. Basic earnings per share grew 5% to 18.10p, helped by 9% fewer shares in issue. Average revenue per retailer was £3,037 per month, up 1% on the prior year. Retailer forecourts recovered from 13.6k at the end of March to 14.3k at the end of September. During the half we returned £330m to shareholders through dividends and share buybacks. We are proposing an interim dividend of 4.2 pence per share, an 11% increase on the prior year. Our October management accounts show Deal Builder reservations running 11% above September. We maintain our guidance for Group operating profit of £395m to £415m for financial year 2027, with accelerating revenue growth in the second half. We will bring the November trading position to the next meeting.
