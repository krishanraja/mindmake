# The ladder: from humans to the business

Status: Working pack. Every rung names its owner; read the owner live before relying on a number.

## The top: for humans and society

**Krish's purpose** (locked; ai-harness `skills/krish-principles/references/who-krish-is.md`): "I see what is coming before it is obvious and make it legible to people while it still counts."

**What the world needs, in his words** (ikigai v4, quoted in the mission brief "Who decides v2" in his Drive): "People keeping their own judgement and edge in the AI era, rather than having it absorbed into a company brain." His first-ranked mission line: "People keep their own minds." And: "AI is the era. My domain is human agency, and AI is where that fight is now."

**The stake, in his words (17 November 2026):** "If you don't master AI, it will master you. If you don't have human agency and accountability, you will cede your decision-making to AI. You will not notice when it's pulling the wool over your eyes, and it will learn your thinking patterns. Companies will build institutional brains that simply replace you."

How it is said in public (Ruling, Krish, 2026-11-17; growth-mindmake `docs/RULINGS.md`, "Who decides"): institutional brains replacing leaders is said only as **the direction of travel**, never as fact, because no study shows it for leaders yet. Customer-facing copy sells ambition and urgency, never fear of job loss (ai-harness `skills/mindmake`).
