# Mindmaker commercial reference

## What Mindmaker is

Mindmaker is Krish Raja's commercial decision practice. It helps founders and business leaders make hard product, price, sales and company decisions as AI changes their market. The work protects the expertise, judgement and taste already inside the business, then uses AI to make those strengths more useful.

It is not an automation shop, a training course or an open-ended consultancy.

## The offer

There is one public paid engagement: a focused 21-day Sprint.

The client brings an important problem. It may still be unclear. Krish studies the business, interviews the right people, checks outside facts, separates facts from guesses and works with the team on the first real action.

The scope and fee are agreed after a fit call. No price is published on the website.

CTRL is the private workspace that holds the decision, evidence and reasoning. The client keeps it. CTRL is not sold as a separate offer on this site.

## Best-fit moments

- Faster startups may be taking the market.
- The business could grow faster, but the product, price, position or team is holding it back.
- AI has changed an important product, sales or company decision.
- The leader knows something is wrong but cannot yet name the real problem.

Krish has deep experience in data, technology, media, publishing, content and intellectual property. The headline remains broad enough for other strong-fit businesses, including coaching and consulting firms.

## Buyer journey

1. Homepage explains the business and shows proof.
2. `/sprint` explains the one engagement.
3. Every main sales action says `Book a fit call`.
4. Every sales action reaches the same Calendly page.

Contact is for general messages. Mindmaker Live is the external publication. The interactive Diagnosis Room and AI demonstration are paused.
