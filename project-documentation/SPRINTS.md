## Who Mindmaker Serves

Mindmaker exists for two kinds of leaders. Both are senior. Both have budget authority. Both are drowning in AI noise and avoiding the decisions that actually matter. The difference is how they lead.

---

## ICP 1: The Builder

### Profile

**Who they are:** Leaders who want to build alongside AI. They prototype. They ship. They create leverage by getting their hands dirty. They aren't waiting for permission — they're looking for the fastest path from idea to working system.

**Typical titles:** CEO, CPO, CTO-adjacent leader, Founder, VP of Product

**Experience:** 10+ years in leadership roles. Comfortable getting hands-on with tools, systems, and product decisions. Often the person in the room who opens their laptop and starts building while others are still debating.

### Company Context

| Attribute | Range |
|-----------|-------|
| Stage | Series B to late-stage |
| Revenue | $10M–$500M |
| Employees | 50–500 |
| Budget Authority | $500K+ |

### Their Nervous Decisions

These are the decisions keeping them up at night:

- "What should I build vs buy?"
- "Which tools do I actually commit to?"
- "How do I multiply my strongest edge with AI?"
- "How do I prototype without a full technical team?"
- "Should we build our own AI tooling or use vendor APIs?"
- "I'm nervous about vendor lock-in but scared to build from scratch."
- "How do I prioritize 12 competing AI initiatives?"
- "What's the right first AI project to build credibility internally?"

### How They Talk About Their Problem

- "I've been pitched 14 tools this quarter. I use none of them."
- "I'm either building the future or being replaced by it."
- "Everyone on my team is using different tools. It's chaos."
- "I feel like I should understand this but I don't."
- "I want to build something real, not another proof-of-concept."

### What the Builder Wants From a Sprint

- Clarity on what to build, what to buy, and what to ignore
- Working systems deployed — not slide decks, not demos
- A framework for evaluating tools and vendors independently
- Confidence to defend their decisions to the board
- Speed — they want to ship, not philosophize

### Sprint Fit

| Sprint | When It Fits |
|--------|-------------|
| **4-Week Sprint** | Single build-vs-buy decision. One tool evaluation. One vendor commitment. One system to prototype. |
| **90-Day Sprint** | Multiple systems deployed. Full tech landscape mapped. Team alignment on tools and standards. 12-month build roadmap. |

### What They Become After a Sprint

A leader who builds alongside AI. Working systems, not slide decks. They ship. They know what to build, what to buy, and what to ignore — and they can explain why to anyone who asks.

### Qualification Signals — Strong Fit

- Has one specific AI anxiety they can name
- Board or peers are asking about AI
- Has been pitched by multiple vendors recently
- Wants to make a decision, not learn about AI
- Says "I should understand this but I don't"

### Qualification Signals — Strongest Fit

- Has 2-3 competing AI decisions
- Needs to align a team on AI direction
- Wants deployed systems, not just a plan
- Has budget and authority to act immediately
- Board presentation coming up

### Anti-ICPs (Not a Fit)

- Technical teams wanting implementation help (they need code, not decisions)
- Early-stage founders with no revenue (they need to ship product, not AI strategy)
- Individual contributors (Mindmaker is for decision-makers)
- Leaders who want training or certification (we don't teach, we help you decide)
- Leaders looking for someone to do it for them (we help you decide, not do it for you)
- "Just exploring AI" with no urgency or decision to make

## The Sprints

Mindmaker offers two core sprints. Both follow the Mind Set → Mind Map → Mind Make framework. Both start with clarity. Both end with decisions that stick.

---

## 4-Week Sprint

**Tagline:** One decision. Four weeks. Board-ready.

**For:** Leaders with one nervous decision about AI — whether they're a Builder or Orchestrator.

**Format:** 4 weekly sessions (60 minutes each) + async support between sessions (email/Slack).

### The Four-Week Arc

| Week | Theme | What Happens | How It Feels |
|------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Week 1** | **Relief** | Name what you're actually anxious about. Not what the vendor deck says. Not what the board wants to hear. The real decision. | Relief — you're finally addressing it directly. |
| **Week 2** | **Momentum** | Map your options. Build vs buy. Vendor A vs Vendor B. Do it now vs wait. Every path gets a trade-off analysis. No hand-waving. | Momentum — you see the decision clearly for the first time. |
| **Week 3** | **Confidence** | Make the call. Document why. Not a 40-slide deck — a decision memo. One page. What you decided, why, what success looks like, what the risks are. | Confidence — decision made, defensible, documented. |
| **Week 4** | **Calm** | Board-ready. Walk into that meeting with a one-pager that answers every question before it's asked. No theatre. No anxiety. | Calm — board-ready, done. |

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Defensible Decision** | One clear decision on your biggest AI anxiety, with documented reasoning |
| **Trade-Off Analysis** | All options mapped with pros, cons, risks, and why you picked yours |
| **Decision Memo** | 1-2 page board-ready document. What you decided, why, what success looks like, what the risks are |
| **ROI Framework** | Framework to measure success in 3, 6, and 12 months |
| **4 Decision Sessions** | 60 minutes each, weekly, with Krish |
| **Async Support** | Email/Slack support between sessions for follow-up questions and iteration |

### Example Nervous Decisions (4-Week Sprint)

**For Builders:**
- "What should I build vs buy?"
- "Which tools do I actually commit to?"
- "How do I multiply my strongest edge with AI?"
- "What's the right first AI project to build credibility?"

**For Orchestrators:**
- "Which vendors do we commit to?"
- "How do I evaluate AI vendor promises vs reality?"
- "What's my AI boundary for this year?"
- "What do I tell the board next quarter?"

### Outcomes

**Immediate (End of Sprint):**
- One clear, defensible decision on their biggest AI anxiety
- Trade-off analysis showing all options and why they picked theirs
- Decision memo (1-2 pages, board-ready)
- ROI framework to measure success in 3/6/12 months

**30 Days After Sprint:**
- 70%+ have presented decision to board/stakeholders
- AI confidence jumps from 3/10 to 8/10
- Stopped dreading AI conversations

**90 Days After Sprint:**
- Decision validated with measurable results
- ROI tracking in place
- 50%+ considering 90-Day Sprint for broader decisions

## 90-Day Sprint

**Tagline:** The full journey. Mind Set → Mind Map → Mind Make.

**For:** Leaders who need complete AI direction — whether they're a Builder or Orchestrator.

**Format:** Weekly sessions (60 minutes each) for 12 weeks + async support throughout.

### The Three-Month Arc

| Month | Phase | Theme | What Happens | How It Feels |
|-------|-------|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Month 1** | **Mind Set** | **Clarity** | Identify the 2-3 decisions that will define your AI direction for the next year. No noise, no vendor theatre — just what actually matters for your business. Cut through the chaos to find the signal. | Clarity — the fog lifts. You know what matters. |
| **Month 2** | **Mind Map** | **Leverage** | Build working systems around your actual workflows. Not demos, not proofs-of-concept — real, deployed AI that multiplies your strongest capabilities. Map your systems, your team, your edge. | Leverage — things start working. You see the multiplier. |
| **Month 3** | **Mind Make** | **Direction** | Measure, document, and prepare your board-ready narrative. Deploy remaining systems. Build the 12-month roadmap. Walk away with everything documented and defensible. | Direction — you know where you're going and you can prove it. |

### Month-by-Month Breakdown

#### Month 1: Mind Set (Clarity)

**Weeks 1-4:**

| Week | Focus | Outcome |
|------|-------|---------|
| Week 1 | Audit current state — tools, vendors, team capabilities, anxieties | Complete landscape map. Honest assessment of where you stand. |
| Week 2 | Identify the 2-3 decisions that actually matter. Strip away noise. | Decision shortlist. The real decisions, not the ones vendors want you to make. |
| Week 3 | Builder or Orchestrator path definition. Map your leadership style to your AI strategy. | Clear identity. You know how you want to lead AI. |
| Week 4 | Prioritize decisions. Sequence them. Create the sprint plan for Months 2-3. | Sprint roadmap. A clear plan for the next 60 days. |

#### Month 2: Mind Map (Leverage)

**Weeks 5-8:**

| Week | Focus | Outcome |
|------|-------|---------|
| Week 5 | First decision: deep trade-off analysis. Map all options. | Decision #1 resolved with trade-off memo. |
| Week 6 | First system build/deploy. Working AI integrated into actual workflows. | System #1 live. Real tool, real workflow, real impact. |
| Week 7 | Second decision: vendor evaluation, build-vs-buy, or governance framework. | Decision #2 resolved. Trade-off memo complete. |
| Week 8 | Second and third system builds. Team onboarding where needed. | Systems #2-3 deployed. Team using them. |

#### Month 3: Mind Make (Direction)

**Weeks 9-12:**

| Week | Focus | Outcome |
|------|-------|---------|
| Week 9 | Remaining decisions resolved. Third trade-off memo if needed. | All priority decisions made and documented. |
| Week 10 | Measure deployed systems. Document ROI. Identify what's working and what needs adjustment. | ROI data collected. Performance documented. |
| Week 11 | Build 12-month roadmap. Define gates, owners, milestones. | Board-ready roadmap with clear accountability. |
| Week 12 | Final review. Builder Dossier compiled. Board presentation prep. | Complete package: decisions, systems, roadmap, confidence. |

### Deliverables

| Deliverable | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **3-5 Deployed AI Systems** | Working, measured, documented. Real tools in real workflows. |
| **2-3 Strategic Decisions Resolved** | Each with a trade-off memo explaining options and rationale |
| **12-Month AI Roadmap** | With clear gates, owners, milestones, and success metrics |
| **Board-Level Confidence** | Can defend every decision to the board, investors, or team |
| **Team Alignment** | Shared standards and boundaries for AI usage across the organization |
| **Builder Dossier** | Complete record of all decisions, systems, learnings, and frameworks — yours to keep |
| **12 Weekly Sessions** | 60 minutes each with Krish |
| **Continuous Async Support** | Email/Slack throughout the entire 90 days |
