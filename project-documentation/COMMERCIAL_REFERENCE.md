# Mindmake commercial reference

## What Mindmake is

Mindmake helps leaders put their best judgement to work with AI. It protects what makes them good, brings the right facts and past choices into view, and turns hard choices into working tools their team can use.

It is not an automation shop, a training course, a chatbot or an open-ended consultancy.

## The two public doors

1. `Build Your AI Brain`. Make a leader's judgement, standards, memory and trusted context easier to use. Help them decide faster, see blind spots and spend more of their time on work that needs them.
2. `Build Your AI GTM`. Rework product, price, positioning and people choices as AI changes what customers can do and what they will pay for.

These are two ways into the same practice. They are not separate product ladders. Both may lead to one privately priced 30-day proof. CTRL is the private product and proof layer that helps the work endure, not a third public offer.

The retired Sprint, Teardown and Handover do not define the current product.

## Best-fit moments

- A leader wants to use AI without outsourcing their judgement or becoming dependent on a tool or consultant.
- The leader's time is being spent below its highest value.
- Important standards, choices and context are trapped in one person's head.
- AI has changed what the business should sell, what customers will pay for, how it should go to market or how the team should be shaped.
- A fast-growing AI business needs sharper product, price, positioning or people choices.
- A strong non-AI business needs to change without losing the expertise and trust that made it valuable.

Krish has deep experience in data, technology, media, publishing, content and intellectual property. The public frame remains broad enough for other strong-fit businesses.

## Buyer journey

1. The site helps the visitor recognise the problem and understand the two public doors.
2. The visitor chooses `Start here` and gives a company website.
3. Mindmake gives a useful company read and recommendation before asking for contact details.
4. The visitor answers one easy question and may request the full private brief by email, confirmed with a six-digit code.
5. The visitor receives the branded proposal on screen, by email and as an attached document. Krish receives a richer private fit digest. There is no public diary or direct Calendly path.

The version-two hand-off is live (Gate E closed 27 August 2026) and remains fail-closed on every error: the visitor always keeps the complete local download. Publication interest is a separate, unticked choice. It records interest only and does not subscribe or import anyone.

## Voice

Use British English and words a ten-year-old can follow. Keep sentences short. Explain any needed technical term at once. Do not use em dashes. Do not use `thesis`; use `idea`, `view` or `plan`.
