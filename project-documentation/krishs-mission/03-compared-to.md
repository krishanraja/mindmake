# "Instead of...": how Mindmake compares

Status: Working pack. The owner is `src/data/compareBoard.ts` (live on mindmake.co, at the close of `/ai-brain` and `/ai-gtm`). Its copy is the approved brief's, verbatim. Its own rules: grades are honest and never "improved" ("a board where every rival scores No reads as rigged"); categories only, never company names; no prices; no fear.

This comparison is the strongest answer to "I already use Claude for that". Its "a chat AI on its own" row says it in the site's own words: "A fast, capable assistant." Missing: "It forgets your standards and tends to agree with you."

## The brain door: "What are you weighing up?"

What Mindmake leaves you with: **"A private AI brain that holds your standards and checks its own work, and sharper judgement to go with it."**

The five tests, in order: makes you better, not just the task faster; leaves you a private AI brain you own; that brain checks its own work; sharpens how you decide with AI; starts from your vision and standards.

| Instead of | Grades on the five tests | They give you | What's missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online AI courses | Partly, No, No, Partly, No | Lessons, templates and prompts. | Nothing gets built on your actual work. |
| YouTube explainers | No on all five | Free ideas, in bulk. | No one checks any of it fits you. |
| A chat AI on its own | No, No, No, Partly, No | A fast, capable assistant. | It forgets your standards and tends to agree with you. |
| A company brain | No, No, Partly, No, No | One place for your people to search everything the company knows. | It's built to keep what your people know for the company. It doesn't make you any sharper. |
| A strategy consultant | Partly, No, No, Partly, Partly | Advice and a deck. | The thinking leaves when they do. |
| An AI automation shop | No, No, Partly, No, No | Today's tasks, automated. | Faster at the old job, and no better at the next one. |
| A transformation partner | No, Partly, Partly, Partly, Partly | A large programme for the organisation. | Built around the org chart, and slow to reach you. |
| Hiring an AI lead | Partly, Partly, No, Partly, Partly | A capable person in-house. | Slow to hire, and your judgement still isn't in the system. |
| Waiting until it settles | No on all five | Nothing, yet. | The gap widens while you wait. |

## The business door: "How would you make the call today?"

What Mindmake leaves you with: **"A live intelligence layer for price, product, positioning and people, and a team that decides better with it."**

The five tests: builds your team's judgement, not just an answer; leaves you an intelligence layer you own; checks each call against live market evidence; starts from your vision and standards; ends with a decision in use on real work.

| Instead of | They give you | What's missing |
|---|---|---|
| A strategy consultancy | A recommendation and a deck. | The method leaves with them. |
| A pricing or positioning agency | A new price list or new messaging. | One answer, fixed on the day it ships. |
| Market research reports | Data on your market. | Out of date by the time you read it, and the call is still yours to make alone. |
| A chat AI on its own | Fast drafts of any strategy. | Confident answers that aren't checked against your market. |
| AI sales and outreach tools | More outreach, automated. | More volume on the same price and the same message. |
| An AI automation shop | Today's processes, automated. | A faster version of the go-to-market you already have. |
| A transformation partner | A programme across the company. | Built around the org chart, and slow to reach the calls that matter now. |
| Hiring a head of growth | A capable person in-house. | One person's view, and slow to hire. |
| The team's gut feel | Quick decisions. | Nothing checks them against what the market is doing now. |
| Waiting to see | Nothing, yet. | Others move first and set the terms. |
