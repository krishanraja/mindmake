# Mindmaker messaging and FAQ

Last updated: 12 August 2026.

## One line

Mindmaker helps business leaders make hard product, price, sales and company decisions as AI changes their market, then put the decision into action.

## Short explanation

Krish brings more than 17 years across data, technology and product strategy, plus two years working deep inside AI. In a focused 21-day Sprint, he studies one important problem, challenges the current view, checks the evidence and makes the call with the client. The client keeps the decision and its reason in CTRL rather than receiving a deck that goes stale.

## Main action

`Book a fit call`

The call lasts 15 minutes and checks the decision, fit and useful next step.

## Common questions

### Do I need a finished brief?

No. An unclear problem is a valid place to start. Krish's job is to find the decision underneath it.

### Is this AI training?

No. The work uses AI, but the outcome is a better business decision and the first action needed to carry it out.

### Is this an automation service?

No. The work does not start with a list of tasks to automate. It starts with the expertise, judgement and taste in the business and the commercial result that needs to change.

### What do we keep?

The decision, the evidence behind it, the work started during the Sprint and a private CTRL workspace that keeps the thinking useful.

### How much does it cost?

The scope and fee are agreed after the fit call. The website does not publish a price.

### Why Krish?

He combines long commercial experience with real AI systems he has built and run. He does not hand the work to a junior team, and the goal is not to make the client dependent on him.

## Words to avoid

Do not use AI literacy, AI fluency, prompt engineering, leveraging AI, AI-powered, future of work, chief of staff, productivity or assistant as the public frame.

Use British English, common words and short sentences. Do not use em dashes. Keep approved quotes verbatim.
