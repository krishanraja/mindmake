# Mindmaker sales playbook

Last updated: 12 August 2026.

## One route to a sale

The website explains Mindmaker, shows the 21-day Sprint and offers one next step: `Book a fit call`.

Use the fit call to check:

1. Is there one important commercial problem to work on?
2. Has AI changed the product, price, sales plan or company choice around it?
3. Can the right people make decisions and introductions during the next 21 days?
4. Is Krish's judgement-led Sprint the right shape?

Do not ask the buyer to diagnose the problem before the call. An unclear problem is often the reason they need Mindmaker.

## Best-fit signals

- A faster startup is taking customers or changing what buyers expect.
- Growth is stuck and the business cannot tell whether product, price, position or people is the main cause.
- A useful AI product exists but the company cannot sell, price or place it well.
- Important knowledge is trapped in one leader's head.
- The same decision returns to the leadership meeting without moving.

## How to explain the Sprint

Krish studies the business, interviews the right people, checks outside evidence, separates facts from guesses and works with the client on the first real action. The work lasts 21 days. The client keeps the decision, evidence and reasoning in CTRL rather than receiving a deck that goes stale.

The scope and fee are agreed after the fit call. Do not publish or invent a price. Do not offer a discount in public copy.

## What not to sell

- AI training or certification.
- Automation of a task list.
- A fractional role or retainer.
- Production information technology work.
- CTRL as separate software on the advisory site.
- The retired Teardown or Handover.
- The paused Diagnosis Room or AI demonstration.

## Proof discipline

Use only the approved proof source. Attendee logos show reach, not clients. Client stories show outcomes. Career references show how Krish worked in past roles. Never mix the three.

Steph's quote needs the existing consent record and must disappear when that record is missing or unavailable.

## Follow-up

Every follow-up must add value. Reflect the buyer's own words, state the decision as it now appears, share one useful fact or question and give the same fit-call link. Do not create fake urgency or chase a person who has said no.
