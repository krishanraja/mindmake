# Research artefacts

**Do not index these for retrieval, and do not treat them as Mindmake business content.**

Two long documents on training language models to reason well. They are general research into cognitive frameworks and chain-of-thought technique. Neither describes Mindmake's offers, prices, positioning, ICP or process, and neither was written to.

They live here rather than in the parent directory for one reason: an agent grounding itself on `project-documentation/` will otherwise pull thousands of lines of unrelated framework vocabulary into a commercial answer.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `LLM_CRITICAL_THINKING_TRAINING.md` | A training manual on critical thinking and advanced reasoning for language models. December 2025. |
| `LLM_CHAIN_OF_THOUGHT.md` | The executive summary of the same work. Previously sat in the parent directory as `EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`, where its title read as a summary of the business. Renamed and moved in August 2026, not deleted, because the research itself is worth keeping. |

If you want to know what Mindmake sells, read `CLAUDE.md` and `../OFFERS.md`. If you want to know why, read `../DECISIONS_LOG.md`.
