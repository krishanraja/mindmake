# September 2026 film library

## What this collection is

These silent website loops make an AI-enabled business visible through connected worlds of autonomous machinery. They are visual cues, not literal demonstrations of Mindmake software or evidence of a live operational interface.

The shared visual language is ink green, walnut, brass, glass, cream paper and restrained mint light. The films contain no people, faces, hands, silhouettes, robots or anthropomorphic AI characters. They use cinematic object choreography rather than explanatory text overlays. The only notable diegetic wording is the small `GROWTH` plaque in the quiet-workshop film.

The six original process loops are:

- a 20-second, 1280 by 720 MP4;
- silent and designed to loop without a visible jump;
- 24 frames per second and exactly 480 frames;
- below 4 MB;
- suitable for muted ambient playback when motion is allowed;
- an illustrative metaphor, not operational evidence.

Those six process loops have no WebM or poster derivatives. Generate those only after a placement is chosen. Do not add claims, captions, calls to action or interface labels inside the footage.

The Archive Engine hero package is already placement-ready:

- a 16.167-second, 1920 by 1080 loop at 24 frames per second;
- silent H.264 MP4 and VP9 WebM sources, both below 4 MB;
- matching JPG and WebP posters taken from the loop's first frame;
- composed with a dark, quiet field on the left for the homepage copy;
- sealed as a mechanical forward-and-return cycle so the join is not hidden by a crossfade.

### `archive-engine-hero-loop-r04-16s-1080p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** A recognisable walnut archive cabinet reveals a precision brass mechanism. A request enters in a shallow baize-lined drawer, the cabinet's records and internal machinery work as one governed system, a prepared folio is presented, and the mechanism resets.

**What it represents:** The Mindmake AI brain as owned working memory rather than a generic AI effect: a leader's context, standards, previous decisions and judgement being selected, connected and applied to real work.

**Best used for:** The homepage hero, the opening of the AI Brain story, or a high-level explanation of Mindmake's flagship product. This is the direct replacement candidate for the current homepage `film-02` hero package.

**Do not use for:** Product footage, a literal interface demonstration, or a claim that the system makes the consequential decision. The scene is a generated metaphor for disciplined machine work.

### `quiet-workshop-growth-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** In a composed walnut, brass and ink-green Growth workshop, the central mechanism draws material inward and prepares two paper outputs.

**What it represents:** A broad view of an AI-enabled growth function at work. Many inputs are processed quietly by one coordinated system and become a small number of useful outputs.

**Best used for:** A Growth or AI GTM overview, a section introducing the idea of an autonomous business engine, or a broad visual statement that several hidden processes are working together.

**Do not use for:** A precise claim about qualification, communications or decision support. The central mechanism can read as a press or stamp, so choose one of the more specific loops below when the section has a narrower job.

### `signals-arrive-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** Separate signal carriers travel through converging rails and enter a shared mechanism inside the connected workshop.

**What it represents:** Disparate market, customer and relationship information entering an AI-enabled business and being organised into one usable flow.

**Best used for:** Listening, research, market intelligence, customer signals, data intake, discovery, or the opening of a process story.

**Do not use for:** A finished output or a completed business result. This film is about gathering and ordering inputs.

### `evidence-connects-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** A precision instrument connects moving signals through mint-lit paths, aligning them around a central compass-like mechanism.

**What it represents:** New information being matched with context, evidence, relevance and existing knowledge rather than being accepted in isolation.

**Best used for:** An AI brain, evidence, provenance, connected knowledge, synthesis, reasoning with context, or explaining why the system's output is grounded.

**Do not use for:** An automated final decision. The scene shows connections being formed, not authority being exercised.

### `opportunities-resolve-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** Activity settles into two glass vessels containing clear green crystalline cores and brass markers.

**What it represents:** A large field of possibilities being reduced to two qualified, inspectable opportunities that are worth attention.

**Best used for:** Prioritisation, qualification, opportunity selection, focus, signal-to-noise reduction, or the point where a Growth system reveals what matters.

**Do not use for:** Revenue already won or contracts already signed. These are prepared opportunities, not completed sales.

### `communications-compose-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** Cream papers travel through a brass composition machine, become ordered pages and rise into prepared transparent cylinders.

**What it represents:** An AI-enabled system assembling useful communications, dossiers or proposal material from the evidence already gathered.

**Best used for:** Content preparation, account research, a customer dossier, proposal preparation, marketing production, or tailored outreach preparation.

**Do not use for:** A claim that messages have been sent or commitments have been made. The system prepares the work; it does not impersonate a person or complete the external action.

### `ready-for-decision-loop-r01-20s-720p-web-sealed.mp4`

**What happens:** Green-lit paths converge on an orderly decision station containing prepared evidence trays and an untouched lever.

**What it represents:** Autonomous work reaching a clear boundary: the evidence and options are ready, but the consequential decision remains with a person outside the frame.

**Best used for:** Human authority, governance, decision support, approval boundaries, control, or the closing beat of an AI-enabled workflow.

**Do not use for:** Full automation without oversight. The untouched control is the point of the image.

## Selection logic for another AI

Use the narrowest film that matches the section's job:

1. The homepage or the flagship AI Brain idea: `archive-engine-hero`.
2. Inputs entering the system: `signals-arrive`.
3. Context and evidence being connected: `evidence-connects`.
4. The best opportunities becoming clear: `opportunities-resolve`.
5. Materials being prepared for action: `communications-compose`.
6. Work arriving at a human authority boundary: `ready-for-decision`.
7. The whole Growth engine or connected workshop as one idea: `quiet-workshop-growth`.

The six loops can form a process sequence, but each is intentionally usable on its own. Do not imply that their filename order is a mandatory narrative order.
