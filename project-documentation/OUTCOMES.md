# Mindmaker outcomes

Last updated: 12 August 2026.

The Sprint is judged by whether it changes a real business decision and starts the right action.

## What every Sprint should leave

- One important decision made.
- Facts, guesses and open questions clearly separated.
- The reason and evidence kept in CTRL.
- The first material action under way.
- Clear ownership inside the client's team.
- Less dependence on Krish after the work ends.

## Approved public proof

The only current source for public client outcomes is `BRANDS_AND_TESTIMONIALS.md`, rendered through `src/data/rebuildProof.ts`.

Use the eight approved stories. Use result-led headings. Do not add old offer labels, private contract amounts or removed percentage claims.

Attendee brands show reach. Career references show how Krish worked in past roles. Neither is a client outcome.
