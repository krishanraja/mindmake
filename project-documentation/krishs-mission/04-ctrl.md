## What it is

CTRL, the Living Brain, live and invite-only at https://ctrl.mindmake.co since 16 November 2026. Its approved short description: "CTRL is your personal AI chief of staff. It turns your judgement, clarity, standards, vision and taste into one living Brain, reads your company's world for you, thinks hard calls through with you, and puts that Brain inside the AI tools you already use." Mechanism line: "Your Brain, inside your tools."

## What it does, in plain words (its own page for anyone)

1. **Four questions:** your company, its field, your role, what you are working towards.
2. **Your Brain:** goals, rules, taste, people, what you want to get better at and more time for. Its guesses are dotted until you confirm them in one tap.
3. **The orb:** say or type what is on your mind; it tells you back what it heard in one line, then offers one action: keep it, think it through, or write it for you in your own voice.
4. **Think it through:** a hard call kept as one choice with several parts; your own rules put back to you as questions; each option with its good side and its hard side.
5. **Today:** two or three patterns across the week's news about your company, rivals and field, then one cited card at a time asking "Did this change what you will do?"
6. **Connect:** your Brain inside Claude or ChatGPT through a private, read-only link you can switch off; import from your AI and from Obsidian; a morning email.
7. **Yours:** download everything, see what changed, delete the account.
8. **You decide.** Not CTRL.

The law under it: "The model reads and writes words; code counts, checks quotes and decides what is stored; the person confirms."
